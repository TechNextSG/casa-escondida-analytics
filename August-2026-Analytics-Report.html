Key Insights & Observations
🏆
On Pace to Tie the All-Time Record
129 clicks in 24 days works out to 5.4 clicks per day — projecting to ~166 for the full month, exactly July's finalized record. On a fair like-for-like basis (first 24 days of each month), August is running +21% ahead of July. Four straight months of click growth: 85 → 108 → 166 → on pace for ~166.
🚀
Strongest 3-Day Run in Site History
Aug 22–24 delivered 11, 12 and 12 clicks — the first time Casa Escondida has ever recorded three consecutive double-digit days. CTR across those three days hit 9.5%, double the industry benchmark. If even part of that momentum carries into the final week, August will set a new record rather than tie one.
📈
Growth Has Shifted from Impressions to Clicks
Impressions plateaued at ~120/day (2,875 vs July's 2,904 over the same window) — but clicks kept climbing anyway, because CTR rose for the first time since April (3.7% → 4.5% like-for-like). After three months of "more visibility, diluted CTR", the site is now converting the visibility it already has. That's the healthier kind of growth.
🎯
Branded Search Keeps Getting Stronger
"casa escondida anilao" now averages position 3.7 with a 20.3% CTR over the current window — up from position 4.8 and 15.2% in the last report's window. The top three branded terms account for 200 of the 434 cumulative clicks. Anyone who knows the resort's name finds it and clicks it.
📉
Position 11.9 — Widest Query Net Yet, Same Pattern as July
Average position slipped from 9.1 to 11.9. As in July, this is not branded rankings falling — those improved — it's Google testing the site on ever more competitive dive queries that start on page 3–6 ("anilao diving" at 59.3, "scuba diving batangas" at 37.5). Aug 18 shows the effect in miniature: the day's average sank to 18.6 and clicks hit zero. Winning even a few of these queries onto page 1 is where the next growth phase lives.
💡
The Transfer Guide Is Still the #1 Unfixed Opportunity
The Manila-to-Anilao transfer guide now has 2,378 impressions but just 33 clicks (1.4% CTR) at position 7.5 — the gap flagged in July's report has widened. It ranks on page 1 for the whole "manila to anilao" travel cluster (62 + 52 + 34 impressions on top variants). The title/meta rewrite recommended last month remains the single highest-leverage fix available.
🌏
Singapore & Hong Kong Remain the Best Converters
Singapore (10.6% CTR) and Hong Kong (11.0% CTR) still convert at more than double the Philippine rate (5.0%) — high-intent regional divers. Spain has newly entered the top 5 with 12 clicks at 9.2% CTR. The US remains high-volume, low-intent: 1,897 impressions at 1.9% CTR.
🔮
September Outlook
At current pace August closes at ~166 clicks / ~3,710 impressions. With the record-setting Aug 22–24 momentum, a full September could reach 175–200 clicks even with flat impressions. The two levers to beat that: the transfer-guide meta rewrite (worth an estimated +30–50 visits/mo) and pushing "anilao dive sites" (position 10.2, 55 impressions) onto page 1.
