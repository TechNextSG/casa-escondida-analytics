Key Insights & Observations
🏆
A New All-Time Record — 179 Clicks
August closed at 179 clicks, beating July's 166 and setting a fourth consecutive monthly record: 85 → 108 → 166 → 179. Since May, monthly traffic from Google has more than doubled (+111%) while impressions have grown +257%. The daily rate rose from 5.4 to 5.8 clicks per day.
📈
All the Growth Came from CTR, Not Visibility
Impressions finished at 3,945 versus July's 3,939 — a difference of six. Every one of August's 13 extra clicks came from a better click-through rate (4.2% → 4.5%), the first CTR gain since April. That is the healthier kind of growth, but it also means the site is now extracting more from a flat audience. Breaking the ~127 impressions/day ceiling is the main job for September.
🚀
Four Straight Double-Digit Days
Aug 22–25 produced 11, 12, 12 and 12 clicks — the first four-day double-digit run in the site's history, worth 47 clicks or 26% of the month in 13% of the days. Week 4 overall (62 clicks, 6.4% CTR) was the strongest week ever recorded, and unlike previous months August did not fade at the end: the final three days ran at 7.7 clicks/day.
🎯
Branded Search Is Now Near-Perfect
In August alone, "casa escondida anilao" pulled 41 clicks at position 4.0 with a 31.8% CTR — roughly one click for every three times it appeared. The three branded variants together delivered 80 clicks, 45% of the whole month, from just 367 impressions. Anyone who knows the resort's name finds it and clicks. There is very little headroom left here; the next growth has to come from non-branded search.
⚠️
Three Pages Take Half the Impressions and 7% of the Clicks
The transfer guide (808 impressions, 8 clicks), best-dive-sites (553, 1 click) and location.html (531, 4 clicks) together account for 1,892 impressions — 48% of the month — but only 13 clicks (0.7% CTR). Two of the three already rank on page 1 (7.9 and 9.5). This is not a ranking problem, it is a titles-and-snippets problem, and it is the single largest pool of recoverable traffic on the site.
📉
The Position Drop Is a Desktop Effect
Average position fell from 9.1 to 12.4, but the split explains it: mobile averages position 7.0, desktop 16.7. Google is showing the site to desktop users on far more deep, competitive dive queries — "anilao diving" at position 61, "batangas diving" at 39.5 — which drags the blended average down without costing a single click. Of the 159 queries Google disclosed, only 11 produced clicks; the rest are page-3-and-beyond experiments.
🌏
Regional Divers Convert 3× Better Than the US
Singapore (11.2% CTR), Hong Kong (10.0%), Germany (13.3%) and Belgium (22.7%) all convert far above the Philippine rate of 3.8% — these are high-intent divers who already know what they're looking for. The US is the opposite: 787 impressions, 18 clicks, 2.3% CTR, the weakest ratio of any major market. Reach spans 92 countries in a single month.
💡
A Competitor's Name Is the Biggest Missed Query
"casa bahia dive resort anilao" generated 52 impressions and zero clicks at position 37.5 — the site's most-shown query with no clicks, and the name similarity is currently working against us. Behind it sit "anilao diving" (35 impressions, position 61) and "anilao hotels" (32, position 22.5). Nothing here is close enough to page 1 to convert today, which is why the fixable CTR gaps above come first.
