Key Insights & Observations
🏆
Best Month on Record — and June's Forecast Was Beaten
153 clicks and 3,748 impressions are both all-time highs. Last month's report forecast 120–140 clicks and 2,500–3,000 impressions for July; actual results came in above the top of both ranges. Clicks are up 80% and impressions up 239% since May — the SEO work done in Q2 is compounding.
📅
Every Week Beat the Last
Weekly clicks climbed straight through the month: 27 → 33 → 38 → 55. Week 4 (Jul 22–30) also had the month's best CTR at 4.7%, up from 3.5% in Week 2. Growth isn't coming from one lucky spike — it's a steady week-on-week build, which is the healthiest pattern to see.
🎯
Branded Search Is Now Top-5
"casa escondida anilao" and "casa escondida" now average positions 4.8 and 4.9 respectively, with CTRs of 15.2% and 11.2%. Anyone searching the resort by name reliably finds it. Together these two terms account for 143 of the 356 cumulative clicks — the brand foundation is solid.
💡
The Transfer Guide Is the #1 CTR Opportunity
The Manila-to-Anilao transfer guide has earned 1,696 impressions but only 27 clicks (1.6% CTR) — despite ranking at position 7.6. That's a page ranking well that people aren't clicking. A stronger title tag and meta description on this one page is the single highest-leverage fix available, worth an estimated 30–50 extra visits a month.
📉
Position Slipped to 9.1 — and That's Not a Ranking Loss
Average position moved from 8.6 to 9.1. This is not existing pages falling; it's Google adding Casa Escondida to results for many new competitive dive queries where the site starts on page 3–4 ("anilao dive center" at 31.8, "scuba diving batangas" at 39.8). Those deep new entries pull the average down while branded terms actually improved.
🌏
Singapore & Hong Kong Convert Best
The Philippines drives volume (171 clicks) but Singapore (12.8% CTR) and Hong Kong (13.5% CTR) convert more than twice as well as the local market — these are high-intent regional divers with the budget to travel. The US shows 1,459 impressions at just 1.7% CTR, so that traffic is mostly informational, not booking-ready.
📱
Mobile Ranks Better but Clicks Less
Mobile averages position 6.2 versus desktop's 11.7 — far better placement — yet still converts lower (4.7% vs 5.3%). Better visibility with weaker clickthrough usually points to the mobile snippet or page title being truncated or unappealing on small screens. Worth reviewing mobile titles and above-the-fold content.
🔮
August Outlook
July 12 set a single-day impressions record (227) and Week 4 averaged 129 impressions/day. If the current trajectory holds, August should land around 4,200–4,800 impressions and 175–205 clicks. Fixing the transfer-guide meta description could push CTR back toward 4.5–5%, adding another 30–40 visits on top.
